# NNNN. Title, as a short statement of the decision

Date: YYYY-MM-DD
Status: proposed | accepted | superseded by NNNN

## Context

What situation forced a choice. Numbers, constraints, what was tried. Two to five sentences.

## Decision

What was decided, in one or two sentences.

## Alternatives rejected

- **Alternative A.** Why not, in one sentence with the deciding fact.
- **Alternative B.** Same.

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, what must now be maintained. Include the tests or
figures that guard the decision.
