  THE TEST DOES NOT APPLY TO THESE FRAMES
    The frame covers too wide a field. Beyond 40 degrees of diagonal the sky's light
    is no longer even: it is brighter toward the horizon and fades toward the centre, like a dome,
    which is exactly the shape the vignetting has. The two cannot be told apart, however good
    the program: any figure that came out would be the sky, not the flat.
    It is not a shortcoming of the program; that is the geometry.

    On a wide field the flat is judged only on the structure tests of the master,
    where the sky does not enter at all.
