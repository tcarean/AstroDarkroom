  STRUCTURE OF THE COMBINED MASTER (A+B)/2 - the part fixed to the camera
    analysis size      : 256 x 256 (after 2x2 + downsample)
    What follows is what was left after everything the rotation could remove was removed. Being
    fixed to the camera, it divides out of the lights correctly - BUT only if it was the same
    when you shot the lights. The lens's vignetting was. A strap
    fallen over the lens, condensation or a crooked lens hood rotate with the
    camera too, look identical here, and are no longer there for the lights.

  INFORMATIVE (not tests)
    vignetting to corner    : 0.76 stops (corner = 59% of centre)
    illum. centre offset    :  7.55 %
        illumination peak = (142, 128)  vs  sensor centre = (128, 128)
        It is the ratio of the gradient to the curvature, so it is not scale-invariant: on a
        lens with almost no vignetting it grows a lot without the error growing,
        and beyond a threshold it falls back toward 0. The amplitude is given by "tilt".
    local spots (dust etc.) : +0.13 % (brighter) at (16, 10)
        The deepest compact spot, after the large-scale structure is removed.
        The sign says which kind: minus = darker than its surroundings (dust,
        condensation, an obstruction), plus = brighter (light leak, reflection).
        Above 2 % a warning follows; below that the figure is informative only.

  SCALE of the five deviation tests above
    under 2 %    perfect quality
    2 - 10 %     usable; background extraction removes what is left
    10 - 20 %    usable, but confirm with --light before a hard stretch
    over 20 %    redo the flats
    The most important one is "residual (radial model)": it measures directly how much of
    the frame a purely radial model does NOT explain. It is an UPPER BOUND of the
    flat's error, not the error itself - it holds both the part fixed to the camera
    (the optics; divided out of the lights correctly) and the part fixed in the world (the
    illumination; it does not repeat). The --ratio mode separates them. The corner spread,
    the azimuthal asymmetry and the tilt are partial views of the same deviation.
    "Worst spot" catches what the others miss: a compact defect - a small leak,
    a condensation spot - that the residual clips as an extreme.

  VERDICT: ACCEPTABLE
    Usable, but run the --light mode on a calibrated light before
    a hard stretch, to confirm that the flat really corrects.
