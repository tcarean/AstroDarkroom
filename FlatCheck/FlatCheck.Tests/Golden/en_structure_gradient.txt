    The most important one is "residual (radial model)": it says directly how much of
    the frame does not look like a usual vignetting, symmetric about the centre. It is an
    UPPER BOUND of the flat's error, not the error itself: it holds both what belongs to the
    camera (the optics, divided out of the lights correctly) and what belongs to the world
    (the illumination, which does not repeat); one master cannot tell them apart. The corner
    spread, the azimuthal asymmetry and the tilt are pieces of the same deviation.
    "Worst spot" catches what the others miss: a small compact defect, a light leak,
    a condensation spot, which the residual sets aside as an extreme.
