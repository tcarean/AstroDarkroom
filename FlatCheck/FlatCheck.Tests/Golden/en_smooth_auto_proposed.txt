    scale  300 px : texture left 0.00 %, asymmetry +0.01, tilt +0.03, removed 0.11 % ptp  -> fits
    scale  600 px : texture left 0.00 %, asymmetry +0.00, tilt +0.02, removed 0.11 % ptp  -> fits

  PROPOSED SCALE: 150 px
    The smallest step at which the texture left falls under 0.05 % without
    the azimuthal asymmetry or the tilt falling by more than 0.5 points - the two
    tests the texture does not touch, so a fall there is real structure leaving.

    The rule proposes, it does not decide: it does not know what is the lens's and what is the source's - the hot spot of
    an LCD looks the same as a ring of the lens - and it is blind to a real structure that sits only
    in the corners, outside the inscribed circle. Look at the map of the removed component before you press.
