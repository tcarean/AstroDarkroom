## The fixes, one entry at a time (requirement, failing test, code)

### CR-011 The corner shift uses the session's exposure
- R-023: the corner displacement is the rotation rate over the median exposure of the session
  (the one the drift per exposure uses); no figure when no frame carries an exposure.
- Tests in `SeriesAnalyzerTests` (`R023_...`): a first frame of 0.2 s among 120 s frames gives
  the corner figure of 120 s (600 times too small today); frames without an exposure give null
  (0 today); the report row then has no corner part.
- Code: `SeriesAnalyzer.Analyze`, the `corner` lines (`medianExposure` moves above them or is
  read where it already is); the `<param>` of `CornerShiftPerExposurePx` ("0 when unknown").

### CR-012 A frame too short to stack casts no vote (the owner's answer 1)
- R-085 and R-025: the voters are the trailed frames that are not too short; R-085's sentence
  is made plain ("trailed or not, it casts no vote").
- Test: the fixture `ShortAndTrailed()` (one 120 s and one 0.2 s frame at 88 degrees) has one
  voter, under `Th.TrailAxisMinFrames`: the RA axis is unknown and there is no trail angle
  (axis Y today). A second case with two long trailed frames and the short one keeps Y.
- Code: `SeriesAnalyzer.Analyze`, the `angles` filter gets `!shortExposure[i]`.

### CR-014 The files are refused among the frames (the owner's answer 2)
- R-028 and R-032: the rule of answer 2 with texts A; the console refuses before measuring.
- Tests: `SeriesTests` (`R028_...`): `SeriesOutputs.WriteAll` into `lights\`, `biases\`,
  `darks\`, a subfolder of `lights\`, and the root of a session without `lights\` throws and
  leaves no new file; a new folder inside that root, and the root of a session with `lights\`,
  are written. `AcceptanceTests`: `--out <session>\lights` prints the error, exits 1, writes
  nothing. `MainViewModelTests` (`R032_...`): `vm.Export(f.LightsDir)` gives the `EROARE` line
  and no file. Both languages for the text; the new texts through `PlainWords`.
- Code: `SeriesOutputs`: a check (`RefusalFor(SessionFolder, dir)`, full paths, trailing
  separators trimmed, case ignored on Windows) called first by `WriteAll`, throwing
  `InvalidOperationException`; `SeriesOptions` gets an optional `OutputFolder`, which
  `SeriesRun.Run` checks right after `SessionFolder.Open`, so the console refuses at once;
  `Program.RunIn` passes `o.Out`; `MainViewModel.Export` catches the new exception into its
  status line. The existing tests that "cannot fail" keep their checks and gain the above.

### CR-015 A late failure costs one frame or the plate solve, not the run
- R-026 (and R-025): a frame whose alignment fails for any reason is `could not be aligned`;
  a FITS at the root that cannot be read, for any reason, is skipped as the plate-solve source;
  the report is written from what was measured. Both leave a line in the log.
- Tests: `RegistrationTests`: star lists that make the fit's equations singular (every star at
  one point) give a failed result (a `NullReferenceException` today). `SeriesTests` or
  `LoggingTests`: a FITS at the session root the user may not read (a deny rule set on the
  file by the test, Windows only) and the series still gives its report (ends with
  `UnauthorizedAccessException` today). If the test cannot set such a rule on this machine, the
  header read gets a small seam instead and the plan's deviations say so.
- Code: `Registration.Align` returns a failed result when `FitSimilarity` has no solution
  (`Stats.cs` is a copied file and stays); the registration loop of `SeriesRun.Run` goes
  through a small guarded helper that logs at Warn and gives a failed result, as the measuring
  loop does; `SeriesRun.ReadWcs` catches everything, as `ReadNoise` beside it does.
- Not changed: which exception types the console and the window catch around the whole run.

### CR-016 A wrong pixel size is marked in the window
- R-029: says how it is marked (red border, tooltip B).
- Test in `WindowSmokeTests` (`R029_...`): the window rendered with `PixelText = "4,5"`: the
