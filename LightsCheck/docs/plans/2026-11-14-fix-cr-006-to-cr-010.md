## The fixes, one entry at a time (requirement, failing test, code)

### CR-006 The pattern's strength on a short session
- R-046: the median's own noise is the variance of (odd minus even) times
  `v(n) / (v(n_odd) + v(n_even))`, `v(k)` being the variance of the median of `k` Gaussian
  values as `Raster.Median` takes it (the two middle values averaged on an even count).
- Tests in `PatternAnalyzerTests`: `v(1) = 1`, `v(2) = 0.5`, `v(3) = 0.4487`, `v(4) = 0.2982`,
  `v(5) = 0.2868`, `v(k) k` near `pi / 2` for a large `k`; the share at 10 frames is 0.2411 (0.25
  today); pure-noise crops of 512 px from one random stream, 8, 9 and 12 frames, read under 7 %
  of one frame's noise on every position (fail today: 13.6, 14.5 and 10 %).
- Code: `PatternAnalyzer`: `MedianVariance(int n)` (a numeric integral over the order
  statistics' density, no table) and the `noiseShare` line.
- A formula change: `/validate`. Expected: the pattern's sigma of the measured sessions moves
  by under 2 % of itself (87 frames: share 0.2500 to 0.2539; 82 frames: 0.2500 to 0.2484), so
  no recorded figure should change; one that does is a finding, I stop and ask.
