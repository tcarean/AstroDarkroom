Usage: lightscheck <session folder> [--out DIR] [--threads N] [--pixel UM] [--east left|right] [--north up|down] [--lang en|ro]
       lightscheck <session folder> --inventory
       lightscheck <session folder> --frame N

  The Siril session folder: the frames are taken from lights\ (or from the folder
  itself when there is no lights\), the biases, optional, from biases\. Camera raws
  (.nef, .cr2, .cr3, .arw, .dng, .raf, .pef) or FITS. Nothing is written into the folder.

  No options:   measures every frame and prints the report: focus, exposure and
                noise, dew, trailed frames, drift, dithering, the frames to reject.
  --out DIR     writes into DIR the report, a CSV with every figure and the reject list
                (lightscheck-report.txt, lightscheck-frames.csv, lightscheck-rejected.txt).
  --threads N   how many frames are measured in parallel (default half the cores).
  --pixel UM    the pixel size in microns; without it, the files' XPIXSZ, else the raw's 35 mm-
                equivalent focal length, else 3.92 (Nikon D3300).
  --east left|right  the SynScan arrow that moves the mount east (default right); the other is west.
  --north up|down    the SynScan arrow that moves the mount north (default down); the other is south.
                     The defaults are those of a Star Adventurer GTi with the SynScan Pro app.
  --inventory   the frames' metadata only (no decoding): time, exposure, ISO, pauses.
  --frame N     measures one frame only (1 = the first in lights\).
  --lang en|ro  the language of the texts: en (English, the default) or ro (Romanian).
  -h, --help    this text

Exit code: 0 when every frame was read, 1 on an error or when a frame could not be read.
