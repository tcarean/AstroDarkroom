1. The format to send  [CAUTION]
   - Send the lab an 8-bit TIFF with LZW compression, which loses nothing (0.2 MB). Going from 16 to 8 bits only rounds the values; nothing is lost.
     because: in the rounding a pixel moves by 0.26 of a level on average and by 0.50 at most (a rounding cannot move it by more than half a level)
   - If the lab takes only JPEG: quality 100, with the colour kept at full resolution (the 4:4:4 setting) (0.1 MB).
     because: against the original, the JPEG changes a pixel by 0.58 levels on average and by 3.5 at most; 0.4 % of the values change by more than 2 levels, mostly around level 28 (in the dark sky, where JPEG does worst)
