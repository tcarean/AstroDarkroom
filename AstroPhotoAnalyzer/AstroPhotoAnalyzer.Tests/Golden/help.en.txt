AstroPhotoAnalyzer <VERSIUNE>
Copyright (C) 2026 Tudor Carean

Measures a finished astro image (a stretched TIFF, PNG or FITS) and says
how to send it to print: format, background, colour, size, denoise.

Usage:
  AstroPhotoAnalyzer <image> [options]

Options:
  --medium <baryta|canvas|acrilic>   the print medium; gives the paper's Dmax (default baryta)
  --dmax <n>                          an explicit Dmax, instead of the medium's (e.g. 2.0)
  --icc <profile.icc>                 the ICC profile of the paper on its printer; gives the Dmax, instead of the medium
  --distance <m>                      the viewing distance, in metres (default 1.5 x the diagonal)
  --sizes <cm,cm,...>                 the candidate print widths (default 50,60,70,80,90,100)
  --aspect <w:h>                      the target aspect ratio, e.g. 50:35 (default the native one)
  --target-bg <0..1>                  one more target in the print simulation, besides the 0.05..0.20 grid (strictly between 0 and 1, 3 decimals)
  --passepartout <cm>                 the width of the passepartout (default 7; at least twice the frame, 0 = none)
  --frame <cm>                        the width of the frame (default 3)
  --wcs <file.fit>                    a plate-solved FITS of the same size, whose WCS is used for the dust clouds
  --lang <en|ro>                      the interface language: en English, ro Romanian (default en)
  -o, --out <file>                    also writes the report to a file, besides stdout
  -h, --help                          this text

Exit codes: 0 ok, 1 error, 2 caution, 3 problem.
