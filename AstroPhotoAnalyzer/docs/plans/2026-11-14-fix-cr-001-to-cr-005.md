## The fixes, one commit each (requirement, failing test, code, docs)

### CR-001 The judge decides every pixel (`Analysis/GamutFit.cs`, `Runs/GamutFitRun.cs`)

- R-175 reworded: whether a pixel is outside is the Soft proof judge's answer for the pixel
  itself, for every pixel; the table and the edge search only say where a pixel lands.
- `GamutFitTable` gains the judge from a pixel's own sRGB (`GamutFitRun.Execute` sets it to
  `GamutAnalyzer.Outside`; the tests' tables fall back on `Judge`). One private helper shared by
  `Apply` and `Recipe` answers (outside, largest chroma): outside is the judge's word; when the
  judge rejects a pixel the edge does not (c under the table's or the searched edge), the edge is
  searched again downward from the pixel's own chroma (`ExactEdge`, bracket under c); when the
  judge keeps a pixel past the edge, the pixel is not outside and the hard fit does not move it.
  `OutsideAfter` is the judge on the final colour of every pixel, moved or not.
- Tests: `R175_...` with a judge that disagrees with the table both ways (rejects a pixel far
  inside the table, keeps one past it); the existing R-175/R-176 tests stay as they are.
- The whole-image fit is timed before and after (`paper-fit.md` holds 19.5 s / 27.1 s / 10.8 s).

### CR-002 A grey pixel out of the paper's reach is counted (`Analysis/GamutFit.cs`)

- R-175: a pixel whose tone is out of reach, a grey one included, stays as it is and is counted.
- The grey exemption moves after the judge: a pixel under `GamutFitMinChroma` the judge rejects
  is copied and counted in "before", "after" and "tone out of reach"; the recipe counts it among
  those a mask cannot bring in.
- Test: the existing fake paper (no tone under L\* 8) with a grey pixel at L\* 5.
- Mono stays 0 (the owner's answer): a line in `docs/design/known-limitations.md` with the
  figures above.

### CR-003 A threshold on a whole level is compared exactly

- One core helper (in `Input/RasterOps.cs`): a figure in 8-bit levels against a threshold,
  compared on the 16-bit steps a file can hold (1/257 of a level), so a figure that sits on the
  threshold never passes it by the float's last bit. The figures themselves are not rounded, so
  no printed figure moves.
- Used in `QuantizationAnalyzer.Accept` (range under 6, mean under 70), `EdgeRegion.Flagged` and
  `Coloured` and `Recommendations.ColourOf` (more than 3), `JpegAnalyzer.ErrorAccumulator.Add`
  and `JpegRow.Acceptable` (over 2, at most 10).
- R-080, R-061, R-083 say so; tests `R080_...`, `R061_...`, `R083_...` over every sky level, on
  rasters built as the reader builds them (R-083 moves from `accepted` to `implemented`).
- `Golden/mono-png8.txt` may move (the review says so): its whole diff is read and reported.

### CR-004 A FITS that is not on a 0..1 scale is refused (`Input/FitsReader.cs`)

- R-013: when more than `Thresholds.OffScaleShare` (0.5, new, with the measurement above as its
  comment) of the samples are over 1, the file is refused: `RefusedImageException`, so the
  console prints `ERROR:` and exits 1 and the window shows its error card, as for a linear image.
- The new text, for the owner's eye (there is no old one; today the file is read as white):
  - English: `the file's values are not between 0 and 1: 98.7 % of them are over 1 (the largest
    is 65535). The tool reads a FITS on a scale from 0 to 1. Save it from Siril as 32-bit on a
    0 to 1 scale, or as 16-bit, and analyse that file.`
  - Romanian: `valorile din fisier nu sunt intre 0 si 1: 98.7 % din ele trec de 1 (cea mai mare
    e 65535). Unealta citeste un FITS pe o scara de la 0 la 1. Salveaza-l din Siril pe 32 de
    biti pe scara 0..1, sau pe 16 biti, si analizeaza fisierul acela.`
- Tests: `R013_...` in `FitsReaderTests` (a float FITS on 0..65535 is refused; one with a few
  values over 1 is still clipped and noted) and one in `CliTests` (exit 1, nothing on stdout).
- README: one line where the limits a user meets are listed.

### CR-005 Opening another image drops the previous result (`Wpf/ViewModels/MainViewModel.cs`)

- R-111: opening another image drops the previous image's result at once, even when a bad field

- `OpenAsync`: when the path changes and no run lasts, `_result` is dropped, `HasResult` goes
