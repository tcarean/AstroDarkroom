## The five fixes

Each: requirement, failing test, code, design note, ADR, CHANGELOG line, gate, commit.

### CR-011 FITS darks take their black only from biases of their own size

- Requirement: R-020 and R-041: the black of FITS darks is the biases' only when the biases
  have the darks' size; biases of another size give the darks nothing, the black is then the
  first dark's median (`the black taken from the average value of frame 1`).
- Code: `Analysis/FrameStack.cs` (`Load`): the black handed in comes with the size of the group
  it was read on and is used only when the stack has that size. `Runs/DarkSeriesRun.cs` passes
  the biases' size with their black. The later size check (`BiasAnalyzer.DarkMinusBias`) and
  its WARNING stay.
- Test: `R041_FitsDarksDoNotTakeTheirBlackFromBiasesOfAnotherSize`: darks at pedestal 200,
  biases of 128 x 96 at pedestal 1000: the darks' black is about 200, the header names frame 1,
  the hot pixels are counted (today: black 1000, none).
- No ADR (R-041 already says "left out of every figure"). Note: `docs/design/dark-analysis.md`.

### CR-012 The stack takes the setting most frames have

- Requirement: R-020 (and R-007's sentence on the dominant setting): a group's frames are kept
  when they have the group's dominant setting, the one check 1 names (`MatchAnalyzer.Settings`:
  the largest cluster by ISO and exposure within 2 %; the first cluster on a tie). A frame
  whose ISO differs from it, or whose exposure differs by more than 2 %, is left out with its
  error. The size and the scale are set by the first decoded frame that has that setting. The
  same for the biases' stack and the partner's (one `Load`).
- Code: `Analysis/FrameStack.cs` (`Load`): the metadata of every file is read first
  (`FrameInfo.Read`, already done for skipped frames; LightsCheck measured 0.1 to 0.6 s for 32
  to 87 raws), the dominant setting found with `MatchAnalyzer.Settings`, and a frame is compared
  with the first readable frame of that setting through the existing `SameSettings`. The
  normalisation factor stays the first stacked frame's own, so no recorded figure moves.
  When no file's metadata can be read the first decoded frame decides, as today.
- Tests: `R020_AStrayFirstFrameDoesNotSetTheGroupsSettings` (one dark of 1 s, then six of
  120 s: six used, frame 1 left out with the new text; today one used); the same through the
  console's exit code. `R027_AFrameWithOtherSettingsIsLeftOutWithItsError` keeps its frames and
  figures; its two text assertions follow text 1 below.
- ADR 0061. Note: `docs/design/dark-analysis.md`. `README.md`: the sentence on frames left out.

### CR-013 A WARNING when the file names are not in the order of the times

- Requirement: new R-125, as LightsCheck's R-089: the frames stay in file-name order (R-002);
  when a frame with a time was taken before the frame with a time listed before it, a `WARNING`
  row follows the group's `interval` row in the session lines (console and window), naming the
  first such pair. For the darks that are analysed (the partner's when they stand in, R-054)
  and for the lights. Equal times are no disagreement. No figure, frame number or exit code
  changes.
- Code: `Analysis/CadenceAnalyzer.cs`: `Cadence.Backwards` (the first pair, as LightsCheck's
  `FirstBackwards`); `Report/InventoryReport.cs` (`HeaderRows`): the rows, texts 2 and 3. The
  window's session card is fed by `HeaderRows` (`MainViewModel.cs:819`), so it shows them.
  `Logging/LogText` needs nothing if the record prints (checked when written).
- Tests: `R125_...` on the cadence (the pair found, equal times, frames without a time between),
  on the header rows in both languages, on the console (exit code unchanged), on the partner's
  darks of a lights-only run, and in the window's session rows.
- ADR 0062. Note: `docs/design/session-and-console.md`. `README.md`: a sentence where the
  session lines are described.

### CR-014 A block of lit frames is found by a second fit

- Requirement: R-028: after today's fit (Theil-Sen, three passes), a second one starts from a
  line that holds up to nearly half of the frames being lit: among the lines through two
  frames, the one whose middle residual is smallest, then Theil-Sen over the frames within
  `Th.LeakBlockInlierSigmas` (2.5) scales of it, then the same passes. Its result replaces
  today's only when it names more frames, more than `Th.LeakBlockMinFraction` (a quarter) and
  fewer than half of the fitted frames, and leaves a smaller scale. Otherwise every figure is
  today's. Example: eight darks warming 0.8 to 1.2 whose last three carry +0.6 ADU read lit
  frames 6, 7 and 8 (none today).
- Code: `Analysis/LeakAnalyzer.cs` (`FrameLightOf` split into the passes and the two starts),
  `Thresholds.cs` (the two constants). The row, the verdict and the table keep their texts.
- Tests: `R028_ABlockOfLitFramesOverAQuarterOfTheSeriesIsNamed` (3 of 8 and 11 of 30, at the
  warm end and at the cold end, seen failing first); a clean series and the 2-of-8 and 4-of-12
  cases of the existing tests keep their figures (the existing tests unchanged).
- ADR 0063; a dated note on ADR 0012 ("is not tilted" holds to about a quarter of the frames).
  Note: `docs/design/dark-analysis.md`.

### CR-015 The error of a side's constant light comes from the line's own slopes

- Requirement: R-029: the constant part of a side keeps its value; its error is
  `sqrt(scale^2 / n + (mean(ratio) x slope error)^2)`, the slope error half the width of the
  range the pairwise slopes give at one sigma (Sen's interval: with N sorted pairwise slopes and
  `C = sqrt(n (n - 1) (2n + 5) / 18)`, from slope `floor((N - C) / 2)` to slope
  `ceil((N + C) / 2)`). The rule "over 0.15 and over 3 times the error" stays.
- Code: `Analysis/LeakAnalyzer.cs` (`SidesOf`, a helper for the slope error beside `TheilSen`).
- Tests: `R029_AWarmingThatRestsOnOneColdFrameGivesASideAnErrorThatFitsItsLine` (the first
  frame at 0.5 of the master, the others between 1.00 and 1.03, no light: each side's error is
  several times today's and no side is constant light); the existing R029 tests keep their
  sides (their assertions are on the values and on wide ranges; any that pins an error is named
  in "What deviated", not edited in silence).
- ADR 0064; a dated note on ADR 0014 (the formula it states). Note: `docs/design/dark-analysis.md`.
