# Fix CR-001 to CR-005 of the code review of 2026-11-13

## Context

`docs/review/2026-11-13-code-review.md` lists 75 defects found by the whole-code review (ADR
0052), all `open`. The owner asked for the first five open ones to be fixed and marked in the
list, so later sessions know what is done. They are CR-001 to CR-005: all severity 1 (a wrong
figure or a wrong verdict), all `confirmed`, none waiting on the owner.

ADR 0052 says one entry per task; the owner asked for five in one task. So: one branch, one
commit per entry, each with its own requirement change and failing test first.

Two entries had two reasonable fixes; the owner chose on 2026-11-13:

- CR-002: the set that does not say its ISO or exposure is compared as made at the same
  settings as the other, with a WARNING line (R-052's rule, both ways and for the partner).
- CR-005: the quiet pixels are chosen against the master's own level, not against the black.

Worktree `DarksCheck/.claude/worktrees/Fix-DarksCheck-CR-001-to-CR-005`, branch

## The five fixes

Each: requirement, failing test, code, design note, CHANGELOG `Fixed` line, gate, commit.

### CR-001 Sidecar and hidden files are not frames

- Requirement: R-002 gains "a file whose name starts with a dot (the `._DSC_0034.NEF` a Mac
  leaves beside each file) or that is hidden is never a frame".
- Test: `R002_SidecarAndHiddenFilesAreNotFrames` (`._DSC_0001.fit`, `.x.fit` and a file with
  the Hidden attribute beside the darks; the counts and the frames read stay the darks').
- Code: `SessionFolder.FramesIn` (`src/DarksCheck.Core/Session/SessionFolder.cs`) lists through
  `DirectoryInfo.EnumerateFiles` and drops a name that starts with `.` and a file with the
  Hidden attribute. It is the only place frames are listed.
- Note: `docs/design/session-and-console.md`.

### CR-002 A set that does not say its settings is compared at the other's

- Requirements: R-052 (lights against darks, both directions), R-063 (partner), R-060 (the
  INDEX row's ending for a set that does not say its settings).
- Tests: darks with `iso: null` and lights at ISO 800 read a ratio near 1.00 and the WARNING
  row; a partner with `iso: null` against darks at ISO 1600, and the reverse, read near 1.00
  and the WARNING row; the INDEX row of a silent set does not say "worked out for ISO 400".
- Code:
  - `Normalisation` gets `Says(FrameInfo)` (ISO and exposure both known and positive), used by
    `NormOf`.
  - `LightsAnalyzer`: one helper gives a light's factor: the darks' when the light or the
    darks do not say their settings, else the light's own. Used by `Analyze` and
    `NoiseOnPlane` (today each has its own copy of the rule). `LightsAnalysis` gains a flag
    "the darks were silent and N lights said theirs", for the WARNING row.
  - `DarkSeriesRun.MatchPartner`: when one side is silent, the partner is read at the
    session's darks' factor for the shared ratio and the overlap levels; the partner's own
    index stays as it is. `PartnerAnalysis` gains which side was silent.
  - `DarkReport`: the two WARNING rows and the INDEX row's ending (texts below).
- ADR 0053: a silent set is compared at the other set's settings (extends ADR 0004's "1 when
  the files do not say" to comparisons).
- Note: `docs/design/lights-and-index.md`.

### CR-003 The lights' stack noise in the darks' ADU

- Requirement: R-080: the sky and the stack figures are the lights' brought to the darks'
  settings, as check 9 has them (R-024).
- Test: `R080_LightsAtTwiceTheIsoGiveTheSameStackFigures`: lights at ISO 800 with sky sigma
  2s read the same stack noise and the same "times the noise of the lights stack" as lights at
  ISO 400 with sigma s.
- Code: `StackNoise.Of` (`src/DarksCheck.Core/Analysis/StackNoise.cs`) takes the darks'
  analysis and scales each light's sky median and sigma by darks' factor / light's factor
  (the helper of CR-002), and carries whether any light was scaled. Callers:
  `DarkReport.Sections`, `ScaleReport.Figures.Of`. The `sky` row gains a note only when the
  lights were scaled (text below).
- Note: `docs/design/dark-analysis.md` (scale section).

### CR-004 Lights that do not show the dark's hot pixels have no index

- Requirements: R-064 (no lights' index when R-052's ratios sit around zero: no `index
  lights` row, no `lights` row), R-066 (the label then reads `-idx-`).
- Tests: `R064_LightsThatDoNotShowTheDarksHotPixelsHaveNoIndex`,
  `R066_ALightsOnlySessionWhoseLightsShowNoHotPixelIsLabelledIdxDash`. The loose assertion of
  `R052_LightsWithoutTheMastersHotPixelsAreUnmatchedNotUnmeasured` (`IsNaN || <= 0.05 x
  index`, which accepts any negative) becomes `IsNaN`: stricter, as the entry asks.
- Code: `MatchFigures` gets `Absent` (Unmatched with the median or the first quartile at or
  under 0), the rule `DarkReport.TraceRow` and `MatchText` each spell out today; they read it.
  `IndexAnalyzer.EquivalentIndex` returns NaN for it. `LightsLike` and the label follow from
  the NaN, no other change.
- Note: `docs/design/lights-and-index.md`.

### CR-005 The quiet pixels are chosen against the master's own level

- Requirement: R-024: the noise curve reads the crop's pixels less than the hot threshold
  above the crop's median level in the master (not above the black).
- Tests: `R024_ASetWarmAboveTheHotThresholdStillMeasuresItsNoise` (darks at pedestal 230
  against biases at 200: master noise near the plain series', not 0.00, check 9 not OK on a
  zero); a unit test that `Planes.ClippedSigma` of an empty array is NaN, as its comment says.
- Code: `DarkSeriesAnalyzer.NoiseCurve` (`src/DarksCheck.Core/Analysis/DarkSeriesAnalyzer.cs`):
  `quietMask = excess - median(excess in the crop) < hotT`. `Planes.ClippedSigma` returns NaN
  for an empty array.
- ADR 0054: the quiet mask is relative to the master's level, with the reason.
- Note: `docs/design/dark-analysis.md`.

## What deviated

- CR-002: the flag on `LightsAnalysis` is a count, `DarksSilent`, and the partner's is an enum,
  `SilentSide`; "says its settings" also asks for an ISO and an exposure above 0, as `NormOf`
  already did.
- CR-003: `StackNoise.Of` kept its signature. Each light carries its own factor to the darks'
  ADU (`LightFigures.ToDarks`, set where the light is measured), so the existing test of the
  scale section was not touched.
- CR-004: no deviation. With such lights and a partner, the `lights / partener` row still
  prints the chained ratio around zero, under a verdict that says the hot pixels do not show;
  the entry does not name it and it was left.
- CR-005: on the level just under the threshold the old mask read the noise too large on the
  synthetic series (3.05 against 2.99 ADU), not too small as the entry expected; either way it
  was not the frames' noise.
