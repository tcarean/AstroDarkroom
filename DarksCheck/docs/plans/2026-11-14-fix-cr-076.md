# Fix CR-076: a setting a set does not give is taken from the other set, one both give is used

## Context

CR-076 was added to `DarksCheck/docs/review/2026-11-13-code-review.md` on 2026-11-14 by the
reviews of the fixes of CR-006 to CR-010 (merged as 407a873f). Today a set has a factor only
when its frames give the ISO and the exposure, both (`Normalisation.Says`); a set that gives
one of them is compared with another set "as made at the same settings" (ADR 0053), and the
setting it does give is thrown away. Two outcomes:

- darks of 300 s and lights of 120 s, no ISO in the files (a capture program that writes the
  gain): check 1 says a red WARNING on the exposures, and check 2 judges the files' ratio as
  if the settings were the same: `OK ... as it is`, or "the dark is warmer by 8 degrees",
  neither with ground;
- the session's darks of 120 s against a partner of 300 s, no ISO: nothing says that the
  exposures differ, and the verdict can read `OK: the partner can be subtracted as it is`.

The owner chose on 2026-11-14, in chat, the recommended rule: **a setting a set does not give
is taken from the set it is compared with; a setting both give is used.** With no ISO anywhere
the ISOs are taken as the same and the exposures are used, so such a pair is judged as any
pair at other settings (ADR 0055): never "as it is", both exposures named, the advice with
`-opt`, which is the case Siril's manual describes it for. The assumption is said in a WARNING
row. The owner asked for the merge of the previous branch first, then this fix.

Not measurable on real frames: every frame of the sessions of `docs/validation.md` gives both
settings. The case is shown on generated frames only.

marked in the review list.

- Requirements: R-052 (lights against darks), R-063 and R-064 (the partner and the chained
  row), R-067 and R-080 follow from them (the factor a light's noise and sky are brought with).
- `src/DarksCheck.Core/Analysis/Normalisation.cs`: a new `Relative(set, reference)`: the factor
  of one frame's ADU over another's from the settings both give (the gain per ISO stop for the
  ISOs, the ratio of the exposures), 1 for a setting either does not give.
- `Analysis/LightsAnalyzer.cs`:
  - `FactorOf`: when the light and the darks both give both settings, the light's own factor,
    as today, by the same arithmetic, so no figure of an ordinary session moves; otherwise the
    darks' factor times `Relative(light, the darks' first stacked frame)`.
  - `LightFigures.OtherSettings`: `FrameStack.SameSettings` alone again (each setting both
    give, compared on its own): with the factor now following the same settings, the row and
    the verdict never speak of something that was not worked out. The two conditions the
    first review added are removed.
  - The rows' figures: `AssumedSettings`, a light that lacks a setting the darks give;
    `DarksSilent`, a light that gives a setting the darks lack; a new `UnsaidByBoth` (ISO,
    exposure, or both): the settings the darks lack and no measured light gives.
- `Runs/DarkSeriesRun.cs` (`MatchPartner`): the partner is read at its own factor when both
  sets give both settings (as today), else at the session's factor times `Relative(partner,
  session)`; `Other` by `SameSettings`; `Silent` says which side lacks a setting the other
  gives (a new value for both sides at once) and `UnsaidByBoth` what neither gives. The chained
  row keeps the rule of the second review, per setting: a setting that was not used between
  two sets stands, for the third, where the set it was taken from stands.
- `Report/DarkReport.cs`: the texts below.
- Tests (`tests/DarksCheck.Tests/LightsAndIndexTests.cs`), failing first: darks 300 s and
  lights 120 s without ISO, as hot in the files, read a ratio of 0.40 (the dark is warmer),
  `in the files 1.00 times`, the verdict naming `300.0 s against 120.0 s`, the new WARNING row
  and no "as it is"; the same pair with lights 0.4 times as high reads 1.00 and "do not
  subtract the dark as it is"; a light with an exposure only against darks with both, and the
  reverse; the partner cases; the chained row. `R052_SetsThatDoNotSayBothSettingsAreNotAtOtherSettings`
  pins the rule this task replaces and is rewritten for the new one; the tests of sets that
  give neither setting keep their figures, with the reworded rows.
- ADR 0060, superseding in part ADR 0053 (a set needs both settings to be compared on any)
  and ADR 0055 (decision 1: "both sides saying their settings"). Notes:
  `docs/design/lights-and-index.md`, `docs/design/dark-analysis.md`. `README.md`: the sentence
  on files that do not state a setting.
