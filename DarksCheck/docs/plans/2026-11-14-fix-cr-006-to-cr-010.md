### CR-006 Lights at other settings than the darks

What Siril's manual says (read 2026-11-14, siril.readthedocs.io, Calibration): darks are made
"at the same exposure time and ISO/Gain" as the lights; `-opt` multiplies the dark by one
number, is described for another exposure, and needs the bias master too; `-cc=dark` only
looks for the hot pixels in the dark. So the advice: best take darks at the lights' settings;
with the darks at hand, `-opt` or the cosmetic correction, never a plain subtraction.

- Requirements: R-052 and R-062 (the verdict is never "as it is" when the settings differ),
  R-067 (what a plain subtraction leaves is worked out on the files), a new window requirement
  (the mismatch drawn large and red).
- Rule: "the lights are at other settings" when more than half of the measured lights have
  another ISO, or an exposure further than 2 %, than the darks (`LightFigures.OtherSettings`,
  the rule `StackNoise` already uses for the sky row's note). Lights or darks that do not say
  their settings are not "other" (ADR 0053 stands).
- Code, `src/DarksCheck.Core/`:
  - `Analysis/LightsAnalyzer.cs`: `LightsAnalysis` gains `OtherSettings` and the night's ratio
    in the files (the median of each light's ratio over its `ToDarks`). With other settings a
    `Direct` verdict becomes `WithOpt`, for the night and per light (`Judge`), and nothing is
    relaxed. `TracesOf` measures the trace from the files: `|ratio - c| x k` with `c` the
    lights' `ToDarks` (1 at the same settings, so nothing moves for an ordinary session).
  - `Runs/DarkSeriesRun.cs` (`MatchPartner`): the same for a partner whose settings are not
    the session's darks' (and, for the chained row, not the lights').
  - `Report/DarkReport.cs`: `MatchText`, the `lights against dark` row and `TraceRow` (texts
    below). `Report/ReportRows.cs`: `ReportRow.Alert`, set on check 1's `match` row when it is
    a WARNING and on these verdict rows.
  - `src/DarksCheck.Wpf/`: `RowVm.Alert`; `Theme/Rows.xaml` draws an alert row's text large
    (16 px, semi-bold) in the theme's red (`BadBr`) with a red chip. The row keeps its place:
    first card of the Summary tab (check 1) and the check 2 card of the Summary and Lights tabs.
  - The console has no colour anywhere and keeps none: the mismatch is the WARNING of check 1
    and the WARNING of check 2's verdict.
- Tests: lights at ISO 800 against darks at ISO 400, same temperature: the ratio stays 1.00,
  the verdict is not `Direct`, the report has no "as it is", the trace row says the rest left
  by a factor of 2; a partner at another ISO likewise; the window test: the alert row is drawn
  with the red brush and the larger size. The existing R052 test asserts only the ratio, so it
  stays as it is.
- ADR 0055. Notes: `docs/design/lights-and-index.md`, `docs/design/window.md`.

### CR-007 Check 9 without lights judges only the minimum

- Requirement: R-024: without lights the frames needed are `Th.MinDarks` (10); the value step
  no longer decides. `Th.MasterNoiseVsStep` is removed.
- Code: `Analysis/DarkVerdicts.cs` (`EnoughOf`); `Report/DarkReport.cs` (`EnoughFrom`,
  `EnoughVerdictText`; the WARNING "above the file's value step" disappears, under 10 frames
  the existing WARNING stays). The table keeps its rows; without lights a row from 10 frames
  up no longer says "enough" (it cannot be told), under 10 it still says "too few".
- Tests: a series with noise far above a value step of 1 and 12 frames reads OK without
  lights (WARNING today); `R024_TheNoiseOfTheMean...` keeps its figures, its text assertion
  follows the approved text.
- ADR 0056; a dated note on ADR 0051 (decision 3) and ADR 0007. Note: `docs/design/dark-analysis.md`.

### CR-008 Above the cap, exactly the cap is read, evenly spread

- Requirement: R-027: above the cap N, exactly N files are decoded, from the first to the
  last at even distances (file `round(j x (count - 1) / (N - 1))`, j = 0 .. N - 1; the first
  alone for a cap of 1). Example: five frames with a cap of 2 decode files 1 and 5 (1 and 4
  today); 61 with the cap of 60 decode 60 (31 today).
- Code: `Analysis/FrameStack.cs` (`Load`, `SkippedReason`); the help of `--max-frames`
  (`src/DarksCheck.Cli/Program.cs`) and the window's tip (`WindowTexts.MaxFramesTip`).
- Tests: `R027_AboveTheCapOnlyEveryKthFrameIsDecoded` is rewritten for the new rule (the
  requirement changed at the owner's word, the old example with it) and renamed; a new one
  with 7 frames and a cap of 4 (files 1, 3, 5, 7) and 61 small frames with the cap of 60. The
  two tests that quote the skipped text follow it.
- ADR 0057; a dated note on ADR 0007. Note: `docs/design/dark-analysis.md`.

### CR-009 A known mismatch is a WARNING

- Requirement: R-007: WARNING when the exposures are known and differ, or the ISOs are known
  and differ; OK when both are known and match; UNMEASURED otherwise.
- Code: `Analysis/MatchAnalyzer.cs` (`Analyze`), `Report/InventoryReport.cs` (`DarksVsLights`).
- Tests: `R007_AKnownExposureMismatchIsAWarningWithoutIso`, the same for the ISO;
  `R007_WithoutExposuresOrIsoTheMatchIsUnmeasured` stays (equal exposures, no ISO).
- ADR 0058. Note: `docs/design/session-and-console.md`.

### CR-010 Biases at another ISO than the darks are left out

- Requirement: R-041: biases whose ISO is known and is not the darks' are reported in a
  WARNING line, left out of every figure, and the run exits 1, as for another size. Without
  an ISO on either side they are used as today.
- Code: `Runs/DarkSeriesRun.cs`: the check is made on the inventory's settings
  (`inv.Match.Biases`, `inv.Match.Darks`) before the biases are read, so FITS darks do not
  take their black from them either.
- Test: `R041_BiasesAtAnotherIsoAreReportedAndLeftOut` (darks ISO 1600, biases ISO 400: no
  bias figures, the WARNING line, exit 1).
- ADR 0059. Note: `docs/design/dark-analysis.md`. Whether a verdict flips on real files was
  "to measure" in the entry; it is not measured here, since the biases are no longer used.
