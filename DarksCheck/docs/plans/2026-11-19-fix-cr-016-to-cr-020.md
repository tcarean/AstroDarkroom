## The five fixes

Each: requirement, failing test, code, design note, ADR, CHANGELOG line, gate, commit.

### CR-016 A frame with another black or white than the stack's is left out

- Requirement: R-020. The first frame that decodes with the group's setting sets the size and,
  from now on, the black and the white the others must have. A frame whose white differs from
  the stack's by more than `Th.ScaleTolerance` (1 %) of the stack's white, or whose black
  differs by more than the same amount, is listed with text 1 below and left out, like a frame
  of another size. The same for the biases and a partner's darks (one `Load`).
- Why 1 % and not "any difference": some cameras write a black measured in each frame, which
  moves by a few ADU from frame to frame; such frames must stay. No such camera is at hand, so
  this is not measured; 12-bit against 14-bit frames differ by 450 ADU in black and four times
  in white, far over the limit. Said so in the ADR.
- A float FITS: today a frame is taken as "0 .. 1" only when its largest value is at most 1, so
  one value just over 1 makes the whole frame read as ADU and round to zeros. From now on a
  float frame is "0 .. 1" unless more than `Th.FloatAduMinShare` (0.1 %) of its values are over
  1; values over 1 clip at the white. The lights use the same reading (`ToUshort`).
- Code: `src/DarksCheck.Core/Analysis/FrameStack.cs` (`Load`'s `Decode`, `LogDecoded`,
  `ToUshort`), `Thresholds.cs` (the two constants).
- Tests: `R020_AFrameWithAnotherWhiteIsLeftOutWithItsError` (FITS darks of 16 bits with one of
  8 bits, same size and settings: left out with text 1, the others analysed, exit 1 through the
  console); `R020_AFloatFrameWithOneValueOverOneIsStillReadOnZeroToOne` (three float darks on
  0 .. 1, one with a pixel at 1.0001: its median equals the others'; today it reads 0).
- ADR 0066. Note: `docs/design/dark-analysis.md`. `README.md`: the sentence on frames left out.
- Limit kept, as for the size: the first frame decides. A stray first frame of another bit
  depth leaves the others out, each row saying why. Said in the ADR.

- ADR 0067. Note: `docs/design/session-and-console.md`. `README.md`: the `--lights` section.

### CR-018 The master's noise is read on every pair of neighbouring frames

- Requirement: R-024. The noise from one frame to the next is the median, over every pair of
  consecutive decoded frames, of the clipped sigma of their difference over sqrt(2), at the
  crop's quiet pixels (until 2026-11-19: the first two frames alone). The master's noise, the
  frames needed, the table of check 9 and check 5's noise added during the exposure follow it.
  The points of the row `noise after averaging` and the window's noise chart stay as they are
  (the mean of the first n frames against the next n).
- Code: `src/DarksCheck.Core/Analysis/DarkSeriesAnalyzer.cs` (`NoiseCurve`, the doc of
  `NoiseVsN.TemporalSigma`). No text changes; the figures in the texts do.
- Tests: `R024_OneNoisyFirstFrameDoesNotSetTheMastersNoise` (twelve darks of noise 3, the first
  of noise 9: the figure reads about 3, today about 6.7). An existing test that pins a figure
  the new rule moves is named in "What deviated", not edited in silence.
- Figures that move, the owner's way (chosen with these figures shown): `docs/validation.json`'s
  `masterNoise` of V-003, V-004, V-008, V-009 and V-010 and the check 9 figures of V-005, V-012
  and V-013 where recorded; the examples of R-024; `docs/validation.md` gets a dated entry with

  old one. Any other recorded figure that moves: stop and show the owner.
- Golden files: the master's noise and what follows from it may move in the last decimal on the

- ADR 0068; a dated note on the ADRs that state the old figures (0051, and any other found by a

- Requirement: R-026 (and the sentences of R-055 and R-074 that point at it). Nothing is written
  into the session, the partner or the chosen lights' folder, whichever way they were given:
  - a folder of darks given as `<name>\darks` stands for `<name>` (R-061 already labels it so):
    `--out <name>\report` is refused as it is when `<name>` is opened; the same for a partner

    junction or link, a `subst` drive, a short 8.3 name. Where the system cannot tell (a folder
    that does not exist yet), the full paths are compared as today.
- Code: `src/DarksCheck.Core/Report/DarkOutputs.cs` (`CheckOutDir`, one list of the folders

  `src/DarksCheck.Core/Session/FolderIdentity.cs`, after CopyWcs's `FileIdentity` (two calls
  into Windows' `kernel32`; no package); `src/DarksCheck.Cli/Program.cs` (the early check).
- The refusals keep today's texts.
- Tests: `R026_OutBesideTheDarksOfALibraryKeptAsNameDarksIsRefused`,
  `R055_OutBesideLightsGivenAsNameLightsIsRefused`, `R026_OutThroughAnotherNameOfTheSessionIs
  Refused` (a junction made in the test's own temp folder, and the `\\?\` prefix); unit tests of
