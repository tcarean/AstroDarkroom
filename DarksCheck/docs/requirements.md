# Requirements

The source of truth for behaviour. Code follows this file, not the reverse. A behaviour change is
first an edit here (status `draft`), then a test, then code (status `implemented`).

Rules for entries:

- IDs are `R-NNN`, assigned once, never reused. A dropped requirement keeps its ID with status
  `dropped`. Gaps in the numbering are deliberate: each block of ten is one area.
- Status is one of `draft`, `accepted`, `implemented`, `dropped`. `implemented` means a test named
  `R###_...` exists in a test project under `tests/` (`RepoInvariantTests` checks it both ways; it
  reads the sources of the projects it cannot reference).
- Describe observable behaviour, with one concrete example that includes numbers. No adjectives.
- Error behaviour and the exit code are requirements too.

The explanations behind each row (why this way, what was measured) are in `docs/design/` and
`docs/decisions/`; the user documentation of the same behaviour, in Romanian, is `README.md`.
The nine checks of the plan (`docs/plans/2026-10-11-build-darkscheck.md`) map to the areas:
check 1 is the console contract and inventory block below; checks 2 to 8 take the blocks from
R-020; check 9 and the index take the blocks from R-050.

## Console contract and inventory (check 1)

| ID | Status | Requirement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| R-001 | implemented | Every run prints `darkscheck <version>` as its first line and `Copyright (C) 2026 Tudor Carean` as its second, for `--help`, for a session and on a failed run. The version is the `<Version>` of `Directory.Build.props`; there is no `--version` flag. The banner is written before the arguments are parsed, so no option can suppress or move it. |
| R-002 | implemented | The input is a Siril session folder or a bare folder of darks (ADR 0002). The darks are the frames of its `darks/` subfolder when that exists (empty, it is an error naming it, never a fallback to the root, which holds the stacked results). Without `darks/`, a folder that has `lights/`, `biases/` or `flats/` is a session without darks and exits `1` with a message naming the subfolder found; otherwise the frames directly in the folder are the darks (a library) and no biases or lights are looked for. In a session, the biases are the frames of `biases/` and the lights those of `lights/`, both optional (an empty one counts as absent); `flats/` and everything else are never opened. The frames of a folder are its camera raws (`.nef`, `.cr2`, `.cr3`, `.arw`, `.dng`, `.raf`) when there are any, otherwise its FITS files (`.fit`, `.fits`, `.fts`), sorted by file name ignoring case; the two kinds are never mixed. A missing folder or a folder without frames exits `1` with a message naming the folder. Example: the Smida session of 2026-08-12 reports `darks\ (20 cadre .NEF)`, `biases\ (30 cadre .NEF)` and `lights\ (32 cadre .NEF)`; the library folder `2026-10-10-ISO1600-120s-15.8celsius\darks` reports `. (30 cadre .NEF)` and `(lipsa)` for the other two. |
| R-003 | implemented | The inventory prints one table per non-empty group (`DARKS`, `BIASES`, `LIGHTS`) with a row per frame: its index, file name, time of capture (`HH:mm:ss`), exposure in seconds (one decimal from 1 s up, up to four below), ISO and, for darks and lights, the pause before it; a field the file does not carry prints `-`. The metadata comes from libraw on a raw (shutter, ISO, timestamp, camera) and from the header on a FITS (`EXPTIME`, `ISOSPEED`, `DATE-OBS`, `INSTRUME`). The session lines give the camera and, per group, the dominant exposure and ISO with `(toate cadrele)` or `(17 din 20 cadre; expuneri sau ISO diferite)`; the `pauza` column exists only in the tables of the darks and the lights. A frame that cannot be read, or a `.fit` that does not start with `SIMPLE`, prints `eroare: <message>` in its row and an `ATENTIE` line in the header with the count, and the run exits `1`; the other frames are still listed. Example: on the Smida session row 1 of DARKS reads `DSC_0034.NEF          00:48:53     119.2    400       -` and row 2 ends with `1 s`. |
| R-004 | implemented | `--help`, `-h` or no arguments prints the usage, the GPLv3 warranty disclaimer (`RunReport.WarrantyLines`) and a pointer to `LICENSE`, and exits `0`. An unknown option, an empty argument, a bad value for `--threads` or `--out`, or a second folder exits `1` with a message naming the argument. `--inventory` is the metadata-only run; a run without options is the darks analysis (R-025). |
| R-005 | implemented | The console formats and parses numbers with `.` as the decimal separator whatever the Windows culture (`<InvariantGlobalization>` in `darkscheck.runtimeconfig.json`: `0.5`, never `0,5`). `DarksCheck.Tests` runs under the same switch, so the golden texts compare against the console's formatting, not the test machine's culture. |
| R-006 | implemented | The cadence of the darks and of the lights (`Th.PauseSeconds` = 5): the start of the first timed frame, the end of the last (start plus exposure), the span between them, the count of timed frames, the median interval between two consecutive starts, and the pauses: per frame, the seconds between the end of the previous timed frame and its start; a pause that comes out below zero (EXIF times have a 1 s granularity and a 120 s setting records 120.2 s) is 0; a pause above 5 s is a gap, and the line says how many there are and the longest with the frame it precedes (`fara pauze peste 5 s`, `1 pauza peste 5 s (241 s inainte de cadrul 4)`, `2 pauze peste 5 s (cea mai lunga 181 s inainte de cadrul 4)`). A frame without a time or an exposure is left out and gets no pause, as does the frame after it; without any timed frame the line reads `necunoscut (cadrele nu au ora sau expunerea)`. Example: three darks of 119.2 s started 120 s apart read `3 cadre la 120.0 s; fara pauze peste 5 s`, a span of 359.2 s and pauses of 0.8 s, printed `1 s`. |
| R-007 | implemented | The match of check 1, two verdict lines whose first word is `OK`, `ATENTIE` or `NEMASURAT`, except the bias line without biases, which reads `(lipsa)`. `potrivire`: the dominant exposure and ISO of the darks against the lights'; `OK` when the exposures are within `Th.ExposureTolerance` (2 %) of each other and the ISO is the same, `ATENTIE` naming both settings otherwise, `NEMASURAT: nu exista lights` without lights and `NEMASURAT: cadrele nu spun expunerea sau ISO-ul` when either side lacks the exposure or the ISO. `bias`: `OK` with the exposure when the dominant bias exposure is at most `Th.BiasMaxExposureSeconds` (1 s), `ATENTIE` above it, `NEMASURAT: cadrele nu spun expunerea` when none says it. The dominant setting of a group is the largest cluster of its readable frames by (exposure within the tolerance, ISO), named by the cluster's most frequent exact exposure: lights of 118.5, 119.2, 119.2 s read `119.2 s`; a group with no readable frame reads `necunoscuta`. Example: Smida darks 119.2 s at ISO 400 against lights 119.2 s at ISO 400 read `OK: dark-urile au expunerea (119.2 s) si ISO-ul (400) lights-urilor`; the 2026-10-10 library darks (120.2 s, ISO 1600) against those lights would read `ATENTIE: dark-uri de 120.2 s la ISO 1600, lights de 119.2 s la ISO 400`. |

## The dark series (checks 2 to 8)

The method is the Python prototype's (`prototype/analyze_darks.py`, `supplementary.py`); every
constant is in `Thresholds.cs` (`Th`), the mechanics in `docs/design/dark-analysis.md`
(ADR 0007). Figures are in ADU above black; "ADU-eq" is ADU divided by the normalisation
factor ISO / 400 x exposure / 120 s (ADR 0004), so every threshold is the same whatever the
settings; at ISO 400 and 120 s the two are the same.

| ID | Status | Requirement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| R-020 | implemented | The darks are decoded into memory as 16-bit mosaics (the first file sets the size; a file of another size is listed with `dimensiune diferita` and left out); the scale is the file's (libraw's black and white for a raw) and, for a FITS, which carries no black level, the black is the median of the first bias' reference plane when there are biases, else of the first dark's (`negru 200 estimat din mediana cadrului 1`). For each of the four CFA positions of each frame, named from the file's colour order (`R`, `G1`, `G2`, `B`): the median, the clipped sigma (every k-th value so at most 3 million are read; the population std, then three passes dropping what lies over 5 sigma, at least 5 ADU, from the running centre), the mean, the 99.9th percentile, the counts above 10, 20, 50, 100 and 500 ADU-eq (a level is never under `Th.MinHotAdu`, 5 raw ADU, so a short or low-ISO series does not count noise as hot), the saturated (at white - 1 or above) and the dead (below black - 50 raw ADU: a dead pixel does not respond, so it does not scale), the minimum and maximum. A frame whose ISO and exposure differ from the first file's by more than 2 % is listed with `setari diferite de primul cadru` and left out. The quantisation step is the smallest spacing between the values the first frame takes from black - 10 to black + 30 (2 on a D3300 NEF). Example: six synthetic darks of noise 3 with five hot pixels at 15, 30, 60, 150 and 600 ADU read sigma 2.6 .. 3.4 per plane and master counts 5 / 4 / 3 / 2 / 1 at the five levels. |
| R-021 | implemented | Check 2, light leaks: the mean master of the darks, of the biases, and the dark current (dark mean minus bias mean) are binned in blocks of 16 plane pixels and read in nine regions (the centre 40 .. 60 %, the four corners and the four edges, each a tenth of the map). The verdict reads the dark-minus-bias map when there are biases, else the dark master: `OK` when no edge or corner departs from the centre by more than `Th.LeakAdu` (1 ADU-eq), `ATENTIE` naming the region of the largest departure otherwise, as a leak or glow when it is brighter and as a readout or bias offset when it is darker; a plane smaller than one block has no map and reads `NEMASURAT`. Example: darks whose left 32 columns sit 3 ADU up, with flat biases, read `ATENTIE: stanga e la +3.00 ADU-eq de centru (pe dark - bias); peste 1.0: scurgere de lumina sau glow`; the Smida session reads `+0.09 la stanga-sus`. |
| R-022 | implemented | Check 4, noise: every frame's residual against the median master, per plane: the clipped sigma, median and mean, and the banding, the standard deviation of the row means and of the column means against sigma / sqrt(width) and sigma / sqrt(height) of white noise. The verdict is `OK` when no frame's residual sigma on the reference plane exceeds `Th.NoiseOutlierFactor` (1.5) x the median, `ATENTIE` listing the frames otherwise; the line also gives the read noise from the biases and the temporal noise over it, sqrt(sigma_t^2 - read^2) with sigma_t the frame-against-frame sigma of R-024 (dark current and quantisation). Example: a frame of noise 9 among frames of noise 3 is listed; random row offsets of +-2 ADU read a row banding over 3 x white noise. |
| R-023 | implemented | Check 3, thermal drift: for every frame, the median (and quartiles) of its amplitude at the master's hot pixels (above 20 ADU-eq, below 0.73 of white) relative to the master; the series warms by `Th.DoublingCelsius` (6 °C) x log2(last / first). `OK` within `Th.DriftCelsius` (1 °C), `ATENTIE` with the degrees and the minutes otherwise. Example: hot pixels of 100 ADU climbing from 0.8 to 1.2 of the master over seven frames read +3.5 °C in 12 min; the Smida darks read 0.83 → 1.07, +2.2 °C in 38 min. |
| R-024 | implemented | Check 8, how many darks: on the central 2048 px of the reference plane (less on a smaller frame; the line says the size), for n in 1, 2, 4, 5, 10 (while 2n frames exist), the clipped sigma of (mean of the first n frames - mean of the next n) / sqrt(2) against sigma / sqrt(n), sigma being the median per-frame sigma of the plane; the n = 1 point is the temporal sigma (frame against frame, the fixed pattern out); the noise of the mean of the first half against the median of the second; the median master's predicted noise `Th.MedianNoiseFactor` (1.25) x temporal sigma / sqrt(N), the spatial sigma standing in for a single frame. `NEMASURAT` for one frame; `ATENTIE: doar N cadre` under `Th.MinDarks` (10); `ATENTIE` with the frames that would bring the noise under the quantisation step (`Th.MasterNoiseVsStep` = 1 x), ceil((1.25 x sigma_t / step)^2) and at least 10, when it is over; `OK` otherwise, saying that the master's noise adds in quadrature to every calibrated light. Example: ten synthetic darks of noise 2 read every point within 20 % of sigma / sqrt(n) and a master noise of 0.79 ADU against a step of 1; three frames of noise 6 read `doar 3 cadre` and 57 frames needed; the Smida darks read 0.40 ADU against a step of 2. |
| R-025 | implemented | `darkscheck <folder>` without options decodes the biases (when there are any), then the darks, measures everything and prints the inventory header followed by the sections `2. SCURGERI DE LUMINA SI GRADIENT`, `3. DERIVA TERMICA IN SERIE`, `4. ZGOMOT PE CADRU`, `5. PIXELI FIERBINTI`, `6. MODEL FIX`, `7. PIXELI MORTI SI SATURATI`, `8. CATE DARK-URI AJUNG`, each ending with a `verdict` line that opens with `OK`, `ATENTIE` or `NEMASURAT`, and `TABEL PE CADRE` with one row per dark (time, mean above black, sigma, residual sigma, row banding ratio, amplitude ratio, pixels above 20 ADU-eq on the reference plane). Progress goes to stderr (`decodare dark-uri: 3 / 20`). A dark that cannot be decoded is listed with `eroare:` and the run exits 1 (so does a bias that cannot be decoded, or biases that cannot be used, R-041); a frame the cap skipped (R-027) is listed with `sarit (...)`, not an error, and does not change the exit code; a failure inside the analysis prints `EROARE: <message>` on stderr, after the progress line is wiped, and exits 1; `--threads N` sets how many frames decode at once (half the cores by default). |
| R-026 | implemented | `--out DIR` writes, beside the console text, `darkscheck-raport.txt` (the report), `darkscheck-darks.csv` and `darkscheck-biases.csv` (every figure of R-020 to R-023 per frame and per plane, invariant culture) and the binned maps of the reference plane as 8-bit PNG (`darkscheck-harta-dark.png`, `-bias.png`, `-dark-minus-bias.png`), each block a 4 x 4 square stretched from its 1st to its 99th percentile, and prints `-> scris: <path>` per file. Nothing is written into the session: a `DIR` that is the session folder or under it is refused with `EROARE: --out nu poate fi folderul sesiunii sau unul din el` and exit 1, before anything is created. |
| R-027 | implemented | Above `Th.MaxFramesInMemory` (60) frames, or the cap `--max-frames N` sets, only every k-th file is decoded, k = ceil(count / cap), and the others are listed with `sarit (peste 60 cadre, se decodeaza unul din k)`, which is not an error; the first file that decodes sets the geometry, the files are tried in order until one does. Example: five frames with a cap of 2 decode files 1 and 4 and exit 0. |

## Hot pixels and the pattern (checks 5 to 7)

| ID | Status | Requirement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| R-030 | implemented | Check 5: the median master's counts above 10, 20, 50, 100 and 500 ADU-eq, summed over the planes and per plane at 20; the amplitude percentiles (10, 50, 90, 99) of the pixels above 20 ADU-eq. The verdict line is informative (`OK (informativ)`) with the count per megapixel. Example: the Smida master has 443 pixels above 20 ADU-eq (19.9 ADU at 119.2 s) in 24.2 MP, median amplitude 33. |
| R-031 | implemented | Stability: for every pixel above 20 ADU-eq on the master, in how many frames it stays above half that; the histogram, how many are in every frame, how many miss two or more (`Flicker`); the median temporal sigma of the hot pixels against that of quiet pixels (the reference plane read at every 8th pixel); the random-telegraph candidates, pixels of the reference plane whose temporal sigma exceeds `Th.RtsSigmaFactor` (6) x the median per-frame sigma. Example: a pixel of 60 ADU in three of six frames reads a master of 30, is hot, and flickers; the Smida darks read 412 of 443 in every frame, 92 RTS candidates. |
| R-032 | implemented | Check 7: the dead pixels (below black - 50 raw ADU on the median master) and the saturated ones (at white - 1 or above), summed over the planes; `OK` when both are 0, `ATENTIE` with the counts otherwise. Example: one pixel forced to 0 and one to 65535 read `1 pixel mort, 1 saturat`; the 2026-10-10 library reads one saturated. |

## Fixed pattern and biases (checks 6 and 2)

| ID | Status | Requirement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| R-040 | implemented | Check 6: the structured banding of the master per plane, the row profile and the column profile of the excess minus their running median over `Th.BandingWindow` (101), as a standard deviation and peak to peak; the glow is the leak map's largest offset. `OK` when the worst peak to peak is under `Th.BandingAdu` (2 ADU-eq) and the offset under `Th.LeakAdu`, `ATENTIE` naming what exceeds otherwise. Example: every eighth row 5 ADU up on every frame reads a row peak to peak over 2 and `ATENTIE`; the Smida master reads 0.39 on the rows and 0.17 on the columns (the hot pixels clamped), `OK`. |
| R-041 | implemented | The biases, decoded like the darks: every frame's plane figures (R-020, in raw ADU: a bias is not normalised by its own exposure), the read noise per plane as the median over consecutive pairs of the clipped sigma of their difference over sqrt(2), the mean master's level and noise per plane and its binned maps, and the clipping check on the first frame's reference plane: the minimum, the share of pixels below black and the share below black - 2 sigma against the 2.28 % of a Gaussian. A frame that cannot be decoded is listed with its error; biases none of which decodes, or of another size than the darks, are reported in an `ATENTIE` line in the darks header, left out of every figure, and the run exits `1` as for any frame that could not be read. Example: five synthetic biases of noise 2 read a read noise of 1.7 .. 2.3 per plane and 40 .. 60 % below black; the Smida biases read 1.56 / 1.26 / 1.26 / 1.45 ADU. |

## Reading frames

| ID | Status | Requirement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| R-008 | implemented | A camera raw is dispatched by extension to libraw and read undebayered: the visible CFA mosaic at the original ADU scale, the exact white and black levels (the median of the four channel blacks), the colour order from `filters` and `cdesc`, and the shot metadata. The metadata-only load (`LoadInfo`) returns the same shot fields as the full load, without pixels. No field of `RawReader` is a libraw type, so a FITS run never loads the libraw assembly; when the assembly or its native DLLs are missing a raw run exits `1` with a message naming what is missing. A FITS never goes to libraw. Example: `DSC_0034.NEF` of the Smida session reads as a 6016 x 4016 mosaic on the scale 150 .. 4095 ADU (`libraw (exact)`), with 119.2 s at ISO 400 as its metadata. |
| R-012 | accepted | A FITS frame is read by the hand-rolled reader copied from FlatCheck through LightsCheck (ADR 0003): BITPIX 8, 16, 32, -32, -64 with `BZERO` / `BSCALE`, a colour cube averaged over its planes, `BAYERPAT` shifted by `XBAYROFF` / `YBAYROFF` and by one row under `ROWORDER = 'BOTTOM-UP'` on an even height. A corrupt FITS fails with `InvalidDataException` naming the problem. Its tests came with the copy and carry no ID. |
