Usage: darkscheck <session folder or folder of darks> [--against DIR] [--out DIR] [--threads N] [--max-frames N]
       darkscheck <folder> --inventory

  The Siril session folder: the darks are taken from darks\ (or from the folder
  itself, when there is no darks\ and no other session subfolder), the biases
  from biases\ and the lights from lights\, both optional. Camera raw
  (.nef, .cr2, .cr3, .arw, .dng, .raf) or FITS. Nothing is written into the folder.

  No options:   decodes the biases, the darks and the lights and prints the report:
                light leaks, thermal drift, noise, hot pixels, fixed pattern,
                dead pixels, how many darks are enough, the lights against the
                dark, the index and the label, one table for the darks and one for the lights.
  --against DIR another folder of darks (a library or a session): its index and
                the report of the session's darks against it, in equivalent degrees;
                a session with lights but no darks takes its darks as its own.
  --out DIR     writes into DIR the report, a CSV with every figure for the darks,
                the biases and the lights, and the maps (darkscheck-report.txt, darkscheck-darks.csv,
                darkscheck-biases.csv, darkscheck-lights.csv, darkscheck-map-dark.png, ...).
  --threads N   how many frames are decoded in parallel (default: half the cores).
  --max-frames N  how many frames are held in memory (default 60, 48 MB each at 24 MP) and how many lights are measured;
                above that, one in k is decoded and the rest are listed as skipped.
  --inventory   the frames' metadata only (no decoding): time, exposure, ISO,
                the gaps and the cadence of each group, how the darks match
                the lights.
  --lang en|ro  the language of the texts: en (English, the default) or ro (Romanian).
  -h, --help    this text

Exit code: 0 when every frame was read, 1 on an error or when a frame could not be read.
