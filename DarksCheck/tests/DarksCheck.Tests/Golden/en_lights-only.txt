    ADU is the file's unit: 16 bits, from 0 to 65535, with the camera's black at 200. ADU-eq is
    raw ADU brought to ISO 400 and 120 s, so that series taken with different settings compare
    with one another: on this set 1 ADU-eq = 1.00 raw ADU. A quantisation step is 1 ADU, but a
    mean over many pixels is measured far below it, because the noise blends the steps.

    A deviation in the dark master (a light leak, an offset) is not noise: the master is
    subtracted from every light, so the deviation stays whole in the stack however many frames
    you add, while random noise falls with the square root of the number of frames. The dark
    master, the median of 4 frames, still has 1.89 ADU per pixel; a deviation is set against
    that, not against the noise of one frame. On a single pixel, the noise of one dark, 3.0 ADU,
    hides any deviation below 1 ADU; but the ratio of a deviation to the noise grows with the
    square root of the number of pixels looked at together: on a 50 x 50 patch it is 50 times
    larger than on one pixel, and a 1 ADU step with a straight edge shows under a hard stretch
    of a dark sky.

    Against the sky: a light of this session has 500.0 ADU above the black, so 1 ADU is 0.20 %
    of the sky, about the error of a flat; in the stack of the 3 lights the noise per pixel
    falls to 4.15 ADU with the mean (5.01 with the median): 6.4 / sqrt(3) from the lights, plus
    the dark master's noise (1.89 ADU) in quadrature, which does not average out; and an offset
    of 1 ADU in the master stays 1 ADU.

    The thresholds: 20 ADU-eq for a hot pixel; 1.0 ADU-eq on the map for an obvious leak (the
    level of the hand analysis); 0.15 ADU-eq and 3 times the error for constant light on a side;
    0.1 ADU-eq and 5 times the typical deviation for light on a frame. The last two say 'this is
    real light, measured', not 'it shows in the image': at 0.15 raw ADU the effect is at the
    edge of what can be seen, at 1.0 raw ADU it shows; the remedy (the eyepiece covered, the
    series taken again) costs the same in both cases.
